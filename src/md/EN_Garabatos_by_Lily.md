# Garabatos by Lily

Garabatos by Lily is a creative small business created by Lilian “Lily” Camarena. She is based in Tijuana, Mexico, and she does services like window painting, murals and brand activations/events.

Her day to day is working with colors, markers and paint. Her clients range from very small businesses, particular communities, schools, and international brands. Throughout the year, she paints season, event specific, and timeless paintings. She is most busy for events like Valentines Day, Mothers Day, Halloween, Autumn, Christmas and New Years.

We were thrilled to work with Lily, as from our research, we knew that this will be a fun project. We wanted to create a portfolio page that represented her work and passion: creative, colorful, fun and new.

Our first meeting was to explore her ideas, the inspirations and the whole vibe and structure for the webpage based on her own research. We also did our research, we presented to her existing projects from the internet as examples, and did some questions to orient and ground the project. Basically, writing down the requirements of the webpage.

After, the work shifted to designing the webpage in Figma. Lilian kindly shared her branding and images, which we used for our wireframe and high fidelity drafts. Later, we shared the high fidelity draft with Lilian, which she gave thumbs up. 

The following weeks were mostly developing the project, using Github for remote developing, and Google Docs for the written content. For this project, we decided to use React and Vercel. Vercel was used to deploy the webpage, and React for developing. 

On June 8th 2026, we planned the handoff of the project. Lilian and her marketing agent Fer, were satisfied and happy with the webpage we developed. 

We are deeply satisfied with the final result of this project. And we are grateful for Lily in giving us the chance to develop her portfolio webpage and expand her business with online presence.
